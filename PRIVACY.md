# Privacy policy

Hugh & Kettle collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash
reporting, and it sends nothing to its author or to anyone else.

## What the app talks to

Only the amp: a WMI-1 on the local WiFi network, or a USB MIDI cable plugged into the phone. The
internet permission is what Android requires for any network socket, the WMI-1's included; the app
opens no connection to the internet.

## What it keeps

Tones, songs, preset names, loudness corrections and settings, in the app's own storage on the phone.
They are not backed up to the cloud and are deleted with the app. A tone or song leaves the phone only
when you export or share it yourself.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Nearby devices on the local network | To find and reach the WMI-1. |
| Location | Android names the WiFi network the phone is on only to apps allowed location. The app shows that name on screen and asks for location only when you tap to see it. It never reads where you are. |
| Microphone | The loudness match listens to each preset while it runs, to measure how loud it is. Sound is turned into a level on the phone and not recorded or kept. |
| Notifications | The notification that shows the link to the amp is up, with the screen off. |
